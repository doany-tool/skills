# Retainer invoice template

Use the [Retainer invoice template](https://doany.ai/tools/invoice-templates/retainer?utm_source=skills.sh&utm_medium=skill&utm_campaign=retainer-invoice-template&utm_content=invoice-templates-retainer) to keep the recurring fee and any prior-period extras clear on the invoice.

## Confirm the agreement

Ask for the covered period and reference, retainer amount, work or hours included, billing timing, and due terms. Confirm the prior-period time and expenses that the agreement allows you to bill.

## Prepare distinct lines

1. Put the retainer fee on one line with the covered period and brief scope. For advance billing, distinguish the invoice issue date and due date from the upcoming service period.
2. Enter only approved excess hours at the overage rate. Label the prior service dates. Add approved expenses with dates and descriptions.
3. Apply confirmed tax and actual credits or payments to the correct invoice. Show the total and balance due and verify the billing period against the agreement.

## Example

A $1,500 November retainer with three approved October overage hours at $85 and a $40 reimbursable asset gives $1,795 before tax. Label the October and November lines clearly.
