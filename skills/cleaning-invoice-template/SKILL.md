# Cleaning invoice template

Use the [Cleaning invoice template](https://doany.ai/tools/invoice-templates/cleaning?utm_source=skills.sh&utm_medium=skill&utm_campaign=cleaning-invoice-template&utm_content=invoice-templates-cleaning) to replace the filled example or download a blank form in your chosen format.

## Collect the billing details

Confirm the client and billing address, actual service address, billing period, visits completed, per-visit or monthly rate, approved add-ons, and any client reference or purchase order. Use the completed work log to verify dates. Omit sensitive entry instructions from the invoice.

## Prepare the lines

1. For identical completed visits in the same period, use a visit count and the agreed rate. List the dates in job details. Use distinct lines where deep cleans, move-out work, or commercial visits have different rates.
2. Add approved services and supplies separately when they were billed outside the package. Include only completed or otherwise billable work under the agreement.
3. Apply confirmed tax and credit actual payments received once. Return the itemized invoice with the total and balance due. Replace the example customer, dates, prices, and invoice number before exporting.

## Example

Two completed cleans at $115 and one approved oven add-on at $35 give $265 before tax. Name the date of the extra and list both visit dates so the client can reconcile the invoice.
