# Influencer media kit template

Use the [Influencer media kit template](https://doany.ai/tools/influencer-media-kit-template?utm_source=skills.sh&utm_medium=skill&utm_campaign=influencer-media-kit-template&utm_content=influencer-media-kit-template) to enter the verified numbers, choose a design, and download a PDF or Word file.

## Gather evidence and assets

- Get the creator name, positioning, platform handles, content topics, portfolio, and collaboration contact. Ask for permission to use logos, photographs, and campaign samples.
- Capture platform-specific followers, typical views and interactions per post, audience demographics where available, and the analytics date and sample. Identify whether each metric uses recent posts, a selected campaign, or another time window.
- Ask which formats the creator actually offers, plus confirmed rates and usage or exclusivity terms if they want to disclose them. Keep sponsored and organic results labeled when relevant.

## Build the kit

1. Open with a short description of the creator and the audience they serve. Show one platform per row with labeled metrics and source dates.
2. Pick representative examples and describe the collaboration options in terms a buyer can act on. Include one contact method.
3. Check consistency of denominators and time windows. Label any estimate and leave unknown demographics blank. Review the preview for readability and export the chosen format.

## Browser workflow

When the page exposes browser actions, use `create_media_kit` to populate confirmed platform and audience data, then `get_media_kit` to compare the resulting figures with the analytics supplied.

## Example

For a food creator, list short cooking videos and recipe demonstrations as distinct placements. Attach recent median or average views with the sampling window and name which metric is being used.
