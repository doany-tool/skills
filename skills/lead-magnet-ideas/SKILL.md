# Lead magnet ideas

Use [Lead magnet ideas](https://doany.ai/tools/lead-magnet-ideas?utm_source=skills.sh&utm_medium=skill&utm_campaign=lead-magnet-ideas&utm_content=lead-magnet-ideas) to compare formats by business type and expand one idea into a brief for a downloadable resource.

## Choose an audience and outcome

Ask who should opt in, what they are trying to do now, and what useful next action the business can credibly help with. Identify the expertise, source material, or examples the user can contribute.

## Compare and develop ideas

1. Propose three distinct resources. For each, state the reader, their trigger, what the asset contains, and what the reader can do immediately after using it.
2. Match the format to the work. Use a checklist for preparation, a worksheet for decisions, a calculator for well-defined inputs, and an audit for a short self-assessment with actionable findings.
3. Pick one and draft a brief with title, promise, sections, sample item, inputs to verify, and a natural follow-up. Keep the signup promise aligned with the asset. Prepare a PDF outline for a printable format and an input/output outline for an interactive one.

## Browser workflow

When the page exposes browser actions, use `suggest_lead_magnet_ideas` to explore the business type and `get_lead_magnet_ideas` to read the available ideas. Expand the selected option into a brief matched to the reader's task.

## Example

For an HVAC service, a pre-season maintenance checklist can help homeowners gather filter size, check access, and note unusual symptoms before booking. The brief should identify which tasks a homeowner can do and which require a technician.
