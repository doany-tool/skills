# Electrician invoice template

Use the [Electrician invoice template](https://doany.ai/tools/invoice-templates/electrician?utm_source=skills.sh&utm_medium=skill&utm_campaign=electrician-invoice-template&utm_content=invoice-templates-electrician) to assemble an invoice with separate parts and labor and export a client copy.

## Match the work order

Confirm the customer and job location, work order and permit references, the actual electrical license label if shown, work completed, rates, and approved parts and fees. Ask whether a call fee includes time on site.

## Itemize the bill

1. List the service call, additional billable hours at the agreed rate, and parts or wire with matching quantities and units. If the call charge covers the first hour, bill additional time only after that included hour.
2. List the approved permit fee separately if billed to the client. Record the work date and a plain-language job description with the work order.
3. Reconcile tax and amounts already paid, then show the balance, due date, and reference. Verify the license and permit text before exporting.

## Example

A $110 service call includes the first hour. For 2.5 hours on site at $80 per additional hour, list $120 additional labor, then add the approved parts and permit lines.
