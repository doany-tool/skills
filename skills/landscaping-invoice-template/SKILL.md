# Landscaping invoice template

Use the [Landscaping invoice template](https://doany.ai/tools/invoice-templates/landscaping?utm_source=skills.sh&utm_medium=skill&utm_campaign=landscaping-invoice-template&utm_content=invoice-templates-landscaping) to enter the billing period, edit the visit and material lines, and download the invoice.

## Confirm the completed work

Get the service property, dates and billing period, recurring plan or rates, extra cleanup approvals, and the material quantities actually supplied. Ask for a PO or plan reference when the client uses one.

## Write the invoice

1. Group equal-rate visits with a quantity and per-visit rate, or use one line for the contracted monthly fee. Describe the dates the fee covers.
2. List one-off cleanup, haul-away, or materials as separate approved lines. Give materials their actual units, such as bags, plants, or cubic yards. Keep work already covered by the recurring fee out of extra charges.
3. Check the period, property, and visit count. Add confirmed taxes and actual payments, then show the total and balance due before exporting.

## Example

Four mowing visits at $50, a $120 approved cleanup, and three $15 bags of mulch give $365 before tax. Record the month and the cleanup date so the client can match the work.
