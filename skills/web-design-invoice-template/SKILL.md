# Web design invoice template

Use the [Web design invoice template](https://doany.ai/tools/invoice-templates/web-design?utm_source=skills.sh&utm_medium=skill&utm_campaign=web-design-invoice-template&utm_content=invoice-templates-web-design) to edit the project invoice and check the balance due before exporting.

## Match the project agreement

Get the customer, site or project reference, the accepted scope and milestone, and what the contract says is billable now. Gather approved extra work and actual pass-through expenses with receipts when relevant. Confirm deposits or milestone payments against records.

## Prepare the billing lines

1. Describe the work at the same level as the agreement. Use a project fee when sold as a bundle, or list design, build, migration, or delivery separately when separately priced.
2. Add approved extras and agreed costs such as domain registration on distinct lines. Include a reference to the approval or billing period.
3. Apply previous payments once. Show the subtotal, applicable tax, total, and remaining balance. Use the agreed handover and payment terms and verify that the invoice covers the correct milestone.

## Example

A $4,200 project fee plus $200 in approved revisions and a $30 reimbursable domain charge gives $4,430 before tax. A received deposit of $2,100 leaves $2,330 due.
