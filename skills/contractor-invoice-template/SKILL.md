# Contractor invoice template

Use the [Contractor invoice template](https://doany.ai/tools/invoice-templates/contractor?utm_source=skills.sh&utm_medium=skill&utm_campaign=contractor-invoice-template&utm_content=invoice-templates-contractor) to prepare a customer copy with the job details, project reference, and remaining balance.

## Gather project records

Ask for the job address and contract or purchase order reference, billing milestone, approved change orders with IDs, documented costs or rates, permit fees billed under the contract, and deposits received. Include the actual license or business ID where the user supplies it.

## Itemize and reconcile

1. Use separate lines for labor, materials, approved changes, and agreed permit charges. Label the units and the milestone or dates. Give each change order its reference and keep pending changes outside the billed total.
2. Apply confirmed tax according to the user's treatment. Enter deposits and previous payments that apply to this invoice under amount paid. Check against billing records to prevent double credit or billing a prior milestone again.
3. Return the invoice with subtotal, tax, total, payments, and balance. Verify the customer, payment terms, and reference before the owner downloads it.

## Example

A $3,000 milestone, a $240 approved change order, and $60 in agreed permit fees give $3,300 before tax. With $1,000 already received against that invoice, the balance is $2,300.
