# Invoice templates

Use [Invoice templates](https://doany.ai/tools/invoice-templates?utm_source=skills.sh&utm_medium=skill&utm_campaign=invoice-templates&utm_content=invoice-templates) to choose a format, open a filled example, or start editing the template closest to how the customer is billed.

## Choose the billing structure

| Billing structure | Best starting layout |
| --- | --- |
| Completed visits and extras | Cleaning or landscaping |
| Labor, parts, change orders, and permits | Contractor or electrician |
| Dated time at an hourly rate | Hourly |
| Deliverables, milestones, and a deposit | Web design |
| A recurring fee with overages and expenses | Retainer |

## Prepare the invoice

1. Ask for the service period, customer and seller, billing currency, invoice number, line items, taxes, discounts, and actual payments received. Choose a general invoice when the business needs a simpler layout.
2. For printing, use a blank PDF or edit the online version and download a filled PDF. For editable documents, use Word or make a Google Docs copy. For automatic totals, use Excel or a Google Sheets copy and confirm the formulas cover all added rows.
3. Verify that line totals, taxes, deposits, and balance due agree. Choose US Letter or A4 when the selected file supports it, then preview before exporting.
4. Return the chosen template, why it fits, and the fields the owner should fill.

## Example

A landscaping company billing four visits and a one-off cleanup needs separate recurring and extra lines. Choose the landscaping template and record the service dates and property beside the billing information.
