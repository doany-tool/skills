# Service proposal generator

Use the [Service proposal generator](https://doany.ai/tools/service-proposal-generator?utm_source=skills.sh&utm_medium=skill&utm_campaign=service-proposal-generator&utm_content=service-proposal-generator) to assemble the proposal, review the live document, and export a PDF.

## Gather proposal facts

Get the business and client names, the goal in the client's terms, proposed method, deliverables, timeline, dependencies, and the user-confirmed fees and payment terms. Identify who gives feedback and who accepts the proposal. Ask for any missing commercial term that affects the draft.

## Produce the proposal

1. Start with the customer's objective and a short explanation of how the work meets it. Describe the approach in steps the customer can evaluate.
2. List deliverables with quantities and outcomes, then note exclusions, client inputs, and review milestones. Keep optional work separate.
3. Preserve the approved fee, tax treatment, deposit and payment terms as supplied. If the user gave line items, total them and flag unresolved amounts or tax clearly.
4. End with a specific next step for acceptance or discussion. Return editable sections and verify that the timeline, scope, and fees agree before the owner exports.

## Browser workflow

When the page exposes browser actions, use `create_service_proposal` with the confirmed scope, deliverables, and fees. Read back the proposal with `get_service_proposal` and compare the commercial terms with the user's inputs.

## Example

For a photography proposal, specify the shoot length, location, deliverable count, file formats, delivery timing, and any confirmed usage terms. State the approved price and the next step to book.
