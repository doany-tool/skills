# Checklist generator

Use the [Checklist generator](https://doany.ai/tools/checklist-generator?utm_source=skills.sh&utm_medium=skill&utm_campaign=checklist-generator&utm_content=checklist-generator) to edit the steps, review the checkbox layout, and download the checklist as a PDF.

## Define the job and audience

Ask who follows the checklist, when it is used, what success looks like, and which steps require verification. Ask for the job or product reference, relevant handoff, and any known exceptions.

## Build the checklist

1. Organize tasks in the order people perform them. Put one observable action per line and group related actions under short headings. For the online editor, begin each heading with a hash character.
2. Include a check where the result can be observed, such as opening a download on mobile or comparing completed rooms against the agreed scope. Record the responsible role when ownership changes.
3. Add a place for exceptions, who follows up, and completion name and date when useful. Review the paper preview for usable checkboxes and writing space.
4. Return a copy-ready list and explain any required user-specific checks. Apply the user's operational or regulatory checklist for work governed by one.

## Browser workflow

When the page exposes browser actions, use `create_checklist` to assemble the reviewed steps and `get_checklist` to read them back. Check the final paper preview for the order, space for exceptions, and completion fields.

## Example

For a digital product launch, group file checks, checkout tests, and delivery checks. Include a test purchase in the user's approved workflow and a place to record any broken links and their owner.
