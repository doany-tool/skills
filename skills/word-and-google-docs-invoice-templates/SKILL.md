# Word and Google Docs invoice templates

Use [Word and Google Docs invoice templates](https://doany.ai/tools/invoice-templates/word?utm_source=skills.sh&utm_medium=skill&utm_campaign=word-and-google-docs-invoice-templates&utm_content=invoice-templates-word) to choose a blank document for the type of work and paper you need.

## Choose and open the right file

Select a general, visit-based, contractor, electrician, hourly, project, or retainer invoice. Download the Word file for US Letter or A4 as needed. In Google Docs, make a copy to edit. Check the page setup and table width before changing paper size.

## Fill and reconcile

1. Replace every sample name, date, rate, invoice number, and payment detail. Add seller and customer information, line descriptions, quantities and rates, terms, and due date.
2. In Word or Google Docs, work out the line amounts, subtotal, discount, tax, paid amount, and balance independently. If the owner needs formulas for repeated billing, choose the Excel or Sheets version, or use the browser editor for live totals.
3. Compare the printed totals and due details with your calculation. Preview the document on the target paper size, check line breaks, and export a PDF.

## Example

For four hours at $80 with $100 already paid and no tax, enter $320 as the total and $220 as the balance. Verify both in the PDF after editing the file.
