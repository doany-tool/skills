# Landscaping estimate template

Use the [Landscaping estimate template](https://doany.ai/tools/landscaping-estimate-template?utm_source=skills.sh&utm_medium=skill&utm_campaign=landscaping-estimate-template&utm_content=landscaping-estimate-template) to edit the estimate online or start with a blank PDF, Word, or Excel template.

## Define the scope and units

Ask which areas the crew will cover, their measured size, the number of visits, and who supplies plants, mulch, soil, or other materials. Specify what happens to debris and whether follow-up care is included. Keep optional work and material allowances visible.

## Build the estimate

1. Create separate rows for labor, materials, delivery or disposal, and selected upgrades. Use the matching unit on each line, such as person-hours, bags, cubic yards, or visits. Multiply quantity by the user's customer price for that unit.
2. Add scope notes for preparation, access, existing plants, and any approved watering or handover. Distinguish a single visit from recurring care and spell out any exclusions.
3. Review the subtotal, any discount, confirmed tax, and validity or payment terms. Check the preview against measurements and replace sample prices and addresses before exporting.

## Example

For a bed refresh, price six person-hours and twelve bags of mulch as separate rows. Include debris hauling only when it is in the selected scope. Describe the garden area and which existing plants stay.
