# Pressure washing estimate template

Use the [Pressure washing estimate template](https://doany.ai/tools/pressure-washing-estimate-template?utm_source=skills.sh&utm_medium=skill&utm_campaign=pressure-washing-estimate-template&utm_content=pressure-washing-estimate-template) to edit a live estimate or start with a blank PDF, Word, or Excel file.

## Confirm the surfaces and site

- Get each surface, measured area, condition, and agreed cleaning method. Keep concrete, siding, decks, fences, and roofs on separate lines when their scope or rates differ. Confirm whether extras are selected.
- Gather the owner's unit prices and minimum charge. State who provides water and access, what should be moved, any drainage assumptions, and which damaged or delicate areas are outside the scope.

## Build and review the estimate

1. Multiply area by the agreed rate for each selected line. List optional lines separately until accepted. Use currency and tax details the owner confirms.
2. Compare the selected subtotal with any minimum charge. If it falls below, show the agreed minimum explicitly in the quote. The template relies on the owner to apply the minimum.
3. Return the priced lines, included and excluded surfaces, site notes, and total. Review the live preview and replace the filled example before downloading.

## Example

A driveway of 1,000 square feet at $0.18 per square foot gives a $180 line. If the confirmed minimum is $200, show a $200 quoted total before tax and keep the optional walkway outside that total until the customer adds it.
