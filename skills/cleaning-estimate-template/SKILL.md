# Cleaning estimate template

Use the [Cleaning estimate template](https://doany.ai/tools/cleaning-estimate-template?utm_source=skills.sh&utm_medium=skill&utm_campaign=cleaning-estimate-template&utm_content=cleaning-estimate-template) to review the filled example, edit the customer estimate, or download a blank file.

## Define the visit

- Confirm property size or rooms, condition, standard versus deep cleaning, and the visit count or cadence. Ask what the customer expects in each area and who supplies products and entry.
- Record included work by room or package. Put oven, refrigerator, interior windows, and other optional items on their own lines if the owner offers them. Keep unselected extras out of the total.

## Draft the customer copy

1. List the package, quantities, and approved customer prices. State whether the price covers one visit or the specified set of visits. List what is included and excluded in terms the customer can check.
2. Add preparation, access, supplies, or condition assumptions. Flag unknown tax treatment and confirm any validity date or payment terms before presenting the estimate.
3. Review the live document, replace sample details, and export the approved version as a PDF. Keep customer details in the owner's chosen workspace.

## Example

For two recurring standard visits at $120 each and an approved $35 refrigerator add-on on the first visit, list the visits at $240 and the add-on at $35. Show $275 before any confirmed tax and label the service date or cadence.
