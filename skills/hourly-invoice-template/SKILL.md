# Hourly invoice template

Use the [Hourly invoice template](https://doany.ai/tools/invoice-templates/hourly?utm_source=skills.sh&utm_medium=skill&utm_campaign=hourly-invoice-template&utm_content=invoice-templates-hourly) to enter the hours and rates and review the invoice for the selected service period.

## Verify time and rates

Get the customer, project, service period, dated time log, approved rates, and any client-agreed billing increment. Separate billable from nonbillable time. For each rate change, confirm which entries it applies to.

## Build the invoice

1. Convert time to decimal hours. For example, two hours and 15 minutes is 2.25 hours. Apply only the agreed rounding rule and retain the original time records.
2. Use dated line items if the client needs itemization. For period summaries, combine entries that share a rate and keep the underlying log available. Separate standard, on-site, and after-hours rates when they differ.
3. Multiply hours by rate and verify the total against the reviewed time log. Add the PO, currency, approved tax and terms, then display the amount paid and balance due.

## Example

Two dated entries of 2.25 hours and 1.5 hours at $90 per hour give $202.50 and $135.00. Total billed time is 3.75 hours and the subtotal is $337.50.
