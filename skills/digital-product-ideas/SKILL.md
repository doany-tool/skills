# Digital product ideas

Use [Digital product ideas](https://doany.ai/tools/digital-product-ideas?utm_source=skills.sh&utm_medium=skill&utm_campaign=digital-product-ideas&utm_content=digital-product-ideas) to browse ideas by niche and turn a promising idea into a build-and-test brief.

## Find a buyer and a recurring job

- Ask who the user knows how to serve, what repeatable task costs those buyers time, and which assets or expertise the user already has. Ask how they can reach a few prospective buyers.
- Compare three distinct products. For each, name the buyer, the moment they need it, the file or tool they receive, and how they would use it. Say what must be built before the idea can be tested.

## Write the first-version brief

1. Pick one product with a clear use case and a reachable buyer. Specify the smallest useful package, including the core file, instructions, and one filled example where appropriate.
2. Draft a one-line promise and a concrete validation step. Ask prospects to try a sample on a current task or register interest at a stated price. Treat the price and demand as hypotheses.
3. Specify what evidence supports building the full version, what would lead to a change, and what must be licensed or created by the seller.

## Browser workflow

When the page exposes browser actions, use `suggest_digital_product_ideas` with the intended audience, then `get_digital_product_ideas` to inspect the suggestions. Give the user a brief for the selected idea with a concrete demand test.

## Example

For independent cleaners who lose track of quote follow-ups, outline a spreadsheet with quote date, status, next action, and a filled sample row. Ask three cleaners to use it on active quotes and report which fields they actually check.
